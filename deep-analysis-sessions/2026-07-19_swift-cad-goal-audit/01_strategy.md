# Analysis Strategy

## Task structure

The audit separates four measures that must not be blended: final gates, fully supported capabilities, bounded development envelopes, and local implementation activity.

## Key layers

- Situation: mechanically observed catalog, gate, Git, and static-check state.
- Problem: incomplete capability domains and absent final evidence.
- Issue: a frozen catalog denominator and a parametric-only exact-curve model can understate the true goal.
- Solution: inventory-derived capability coverage plus certified implicit geometry.
- Outcome: binary completion on one clean, tested revision.

## Decomposition strategy

- Structural decomposition by geometry, topology, modeling/constraints, shared API, and exchange.
- Contrastive analysis between development evidence and final acceptance evidence.
- Causal tracing from missing exact representation to incomplete Boolean/topology/exchange gates.

## Evidence-report design

The report must show the zero passed gates, the provisional 4/55 supported count, the domain distribution, the open inventory, and the exact-representation dependency. A status table is used for audit detail; bars and a dependency flow expose the dominant completion pattern.

## Chartable data requirements

- Capability counts by domain and status.
- Gate count by state.
- Evidence-manifest count.
- Worktree and revision state.
